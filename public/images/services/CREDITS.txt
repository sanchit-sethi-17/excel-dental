CURRENT SETUP
The live site loads these photos directly from the Unsplash CDN
(images.unsplash.com), optimized automatically by Next.js. This keeps
deployments fast and requires no image storage.

These local .jpg copies are kept as backups. To self-host instead (recommended
before final launch, or to use the clinic's own photos):
  1. Replace any file here with the clinic's own photo, keeping the file name.
  2. In the code, switch the image URLs back to "/images/services/<name>.jpg".
Ask your developer to flip this — it is a small change.
